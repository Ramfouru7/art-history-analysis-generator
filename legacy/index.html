A guided look at an artwork

The Starry Night

Vincent van Gogh · 1889 · Oil on canvas

STEP 01

Describe

Identify the artwork's title, artist, date, culture or historical period, medium, and important visual elements. Begin with what you can observe.

STEP 02

Analyze

Examine how the artist organizes the visual elements. Consider principles such as balance, contrast, emphasis, movement, rhythm, and unity.

STEP 03

Interpret

Consider what the artist may be communicating. Explore possible themes, symbols, intentions, and the historical or cultural context.

STEP 04

Judge

Evaluate the artwork's significance and explain your response. Support your judgment with details you observed and learned.
